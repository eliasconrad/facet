Subscription

Mute costs €0.99 per month or €7.99 per year on Mac and on iPhone, with seven free days first.

iPhone: payment is charged to your Apple ID at confirmation of purchase. The subscription renews automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Manage or cancel it in your Apple ID settings.

Mac: payment is handled by Stripe. The subscription renews automatically until you cancel it; the link to cancel is in your receipt email. Your license key works while the subscription runs. A Mac subscription key can also unlock Mute on your iPhone.

Keys bought before subscriptions existed stay valid for good.

Mute is a self-control tool. Use it for yourself. It blocks what you choose; we do not guarantee that every app or site can be blocked in every situation.
