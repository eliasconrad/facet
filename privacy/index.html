Privacy

Shut for Mac and iPhone. Last updated 10 October 2026.

What we collect

Nothing. Shut has no account, no analytics, no ads, and no Shut server that receives your data.

On your devices

Your sessions, plans, block lists, and hours are stored on your devices. If you use Shut on more than one device, sessions and plans sync through your own private iCloud database. We cannot read it.

Screen Time

On iPhone, Shut uses Apple's Screen Time API. Apple gives Shut only anonymous tokens for the apps you pick; they never leave your iPhone.

Browsers on Mac

With your permission, Shut reads the address of the active browser tab on your Mac only to close sites you chose to block. It is not stored or sent anywhere.

Purchases

Payments are handled by Apple (iPhone) or Stripe (Mac). We receive what is needed to deliver your license key, such as your email address for the Mac license.
